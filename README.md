#  Bike Sales Customer Analysis Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project presents an exploratory analysis of a bike sales dataset using Microsoft Excel. The goal was to understand customer purchasing patterns and identify the factors that may influence bike purchase decisions.

The project covers the complete analytics workflow, including data cleaning, data transformation, dashboard creation, and business insight generation. The analysis is based on a sample dataset and serves as a customer analytics case study.

---

##  Analysis Objectives

The objective of this analysis was to explore how customer characteristics may influence bike purchases and to derive insights that could support marketing and customer targeting decisions.

Key questions explored include:

- Does income influence bike purchases?
- Which age groups are more likely to purchase a bike?
- Is there a relationship between commute distance and purchasing behavior?
- How do purchasing patterns differ by gender?

##  Key Findings

### Customer Profile

The analysis revealed that middle-aged customers are the most likely to purchase bikes, significantly outperforming both adolescent and older customer groups.

Marital status also appears to play a role, with single customers generally showing stronger purchasing activity across several segments.

### Income and Gender

Income is positively associated with bike purchases. Customers who purchased bikes tended to have a higher average income than those who did not.

This trend was especially visible among male customers, who had higher average incomes and higher purchase rates than female customers.

### Commute Distance

Customers with short commute distances (0-1 miles) recorded the highest number of purchases.

As commute distance increases, purchase activity generally declines, suggesting that proximity and travel habits may influence purchasing decisions.

### Regional Differences

When exploring the data by region, some variations emerge. For example, in North America, single customers commuting 2-5 miles displayed relatively stronger purchase behavior compared to other distance groups, indicating the importance of regional segmentation when designing marketing strategies.

---

##  Business Insights & Recommendations

The analysis suggests several opportunities for more targeted marketing efforts.

- Focus marketing campaigns on middle-aged customers, as this group represents the strongest purchasing segment.
- Prioritize customer segments with higher income levels, who demonstrate a greater likelihood of purchasing bikes.
- Develop campaigns tailored to male customers while further exploring opportunities to increase engagement among female customers.
- Use commute distance as a segmentation variable, particularly targeting customers with shorter commuting distances.
- Consider regional marketing strategies, as customer behavior differs across geographic markets.

Rather than relying on a single marketing approach, the findings indicate that customer segmentation based on demographics and lifestyle characteristics can help improve campaign effectiveness and customer targeting.
